A small browser helper for Mac and Windows. Bring fixture listings into Same End and take a whole batch of reminders to your calendar.

Download the helper ↓Open Same End ↗

Set up once · Chrome or Edge

  1. Download and unzip the helper. Keep the resulting Same-End-Local-Fixtures folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents. Moving it later can stop the helper working.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions. In Edge, open edge://extensions. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select that folder.
  3. Open your browser’s extensions menu (the puzzle piece) and pin Same End Local Fixtures.

This test version is installed manually. The same download works on Mac and Windows. It is for desktop Chrome and Edge; Safari and phones need a different route.

Then it’s a couple of clicks

  1. Open the helper and choose Find Aston Villa fixtures for this month’s BBC fixtures. Allow BBC access when your browser asks. Or visit a supported listing page and choose Import this page.
  2. Choose Open Same End. Your matches appear under My matches · on this computer. If you open the website yourself, choose Connect my local matches once.
  3. Choose Add all to calendar. Open the downloaded file and accept your calendar’s import. Reminders default to 15 minutes before kick-off.

You can change your country and reminder time in the helper. Times are shown in UK time, switching between GMT and BST.

Private by design

Imported matches and TV listings stay in this browser’s local storage. Connecting lets Same End display them on this computer; they are not sent to the club diary or Same End’s servers. The helper does not read passwords, cookies or browsing history.

Import is your action, not a background crawler. The BBC shortcut contacts the BBC directly. Source links stay with each match. Check the original listing before buying a subscription: fixtures and broadcasters change.

Reminders and calendars

Desktop reminders need the browser running and notification permission. Sleeping or closed computers may deliver late or miss a reminder. Calendar files include an alarm, but your calendar decides how it is handled. A calendar that syncs may keep its own private cloud copy. After a changed or cancelled fixture, import the updated listing and download a fresh calendar file; some calendars may need you to edit or replace the earlier entry.


What can it read?

BBC football fixtures and supported LiveSoccerTV match pages with recognisable event data. TV channel coverage varies by source and country; BBC fixture pages often have no channel information. Unsupported layouts show a message instead of inventing a match or channel. A page being readable does not grant permission to redistribute its listings: use the original sites under their conditions.


Update, disconnect or remove

This manually installed prototype does not update itself. Download a new package, replace the files in the same folder and press Reload on your browser’s extensions page. Reopen Same End afterwards. Disconnect in Same End stops the website reading local matches; Remove deletes a match from the helper. To remove everything, uninstall the extension on the browser’s extensions page. Other browser profiles or computers have their own separate local data.
