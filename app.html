Privacy & practical help

Share only what you’re entitled to share. Protected video may appear black. Your microphone is never requested.

Invitations are private to the people you send them to, but anyone with the full link can join while the room is available. End the room to close it. A recipient can retain what they receive.

Link joining uses a public WebTorrent tracker and STUN for introductions. These services see connection metadata and network addresses. Video travels between peers; there is no TURN media relay. Direct connections across different homes may fail because of routers, VPNs or firewalls.

Same End is an experimental shared screen. Short clip boundaries may be noticeable. Invited viewers may relay to further friends, with up to two outgoing links per computer; if a relay leaves, viewers try to reconnect while keeping the group clock. A pause is still possible.

For link joining, use the hosted browser app or local launcher. The saved HTML includes the app and artwork, without private club keys or video. Its offline workflow uses manual connection files. Ordinary torrent players cannot play Same End’s live extension.
