A little help improving Same End?

We use browser storage for saved access and settings. Optional analytics count visits and a few actions, without names, room links or chat.

Analytics are off until you allow them. We count page loads, successful sharing starts and room-invitation copies. Daily totals expire after 90 days and are excluded from reports after expiry, without visitor IDs, addresses, device details, chat or invitation content in the analytics records. These are action counts, not unique people.

Same End uses its existing control service, hosted on AWS, for these totals. As with other service requests, infrastructure receives network metadata; separate short-lived security limits use daily address hashes to prevent abuse. Sign-in is separate and its provider may use session cookies.

Your choice is saved in this browser for 180 days. You can change it through Privacy choices at any time. Declining keeps rooms, chat and account access available. Withdrawing stops new analytics; previously combined totals cannot be attributed back to you. Full privacy information
