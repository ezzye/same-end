How the stream works

The stream adapts automatically to the connection between people in the room. When a video highlight plays recorded sound, commentary pauses and can keep arriving while the connection allows. Afterwards, commentary can resume from your saved listening position. This can put you further behind live. Earlier clips can be chosen while they remain available. Choosing an audio clip changes what you hear. If sound is blocked, use Play sound. Card times show when items were shared or received; they are not the match clock. Match minutes, when supplied, appear separately.

    Disclaimer

    Same End is intended to let a pre-existing, limited circle of family, friends and fellow supporters share a live screen as part of a private social conversation. Their connection exists beyond the particular programme or match, through personal relationships, shared club history, friendship or fraternity. The justification is that technology can extend an ordinary shared experience across distance while preserving the social character of the group. The session has no public audience; genuine, proportionate extracts used for discussion may also qualify for fair dealing, depending on their purpose, context and the rights attached to the source.
