Browser basics

What happens next?

This checks local video playback and a few browser features. It does not test a private room, another person, your connection to a friend, or match streaming.

When these checks look ready, open the private Same End invitation in Safari, Chrome or Edge, keep that page open, and try the room with a friend. If an invitation opens inside another app and has trouble, choose Open in Safari or your usual browser.

Keep your screen awake while watching. Phone screen capture and the desktop sharing extension are not checked here. Intended paths include iOS/iPadOS 14.5+ with Safari 14.1+; other devices can differ. Some specific older device and OS combinations still need owner testing.
