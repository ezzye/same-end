Invite your small circle, put the next game in the diary, and watch together.

Open Same End ↗

1. Make your club

Choose Create your club, name it and pick your colours. Choose the club’s Invite your mates and send its member invitation privately. Your mates open it and choose Join & save club.

Keep your separate organiser link for yourself. Your saved club returns on the same browser; use the private invitation on another device.

2. Put a match in the diary

The organiser opens Plan a match. Pick a starter fixture or enter your own match and kick-off in London time. The starter list is small, checked on 21 October 2026, and may change; check its source before match day.

Choose Add to calendar, or enable optional match reminders in club settings. Keep calendar files private: they include your club invitation. Download a fresh entry after a kick-off change.

3. Meet, chat, watch

Open your saved club and chat with your mates. The organiser chooses Share a tab and makes the room invitation ready. When Join live appears in the club, members choose it.

Watch the player in Same End, select Turn my sound on if needed, and keep your laptop awake with the app open. New match shares choose a shared starting delay from observed delivery. Watch the delay shown in the room. Keep the original player unmuted with its volume up. Same End keeps its tab quiet locally where supported. Use Mute my sound in Same End to mute only your own listening. The host gets a warning after 15 seconds of detected source silence.

About the shared delay

The host and supporting viewers follow one measured common starting delay, normally between 30 and 120 seconds when the observations support it. You can prepare the share and send invitations later. The connection check begins when the first viewer connects. It needs at least 20 seconds of useful observations within the next minute. Without enough evidence, that share keeps its conservative start. If the host has already been watching alone, the shorter group start catches up once.

Existing sessions keep their timing. Update the app and start a fresh share; saved club and combined room invitations stay the same. Individual viewers may build up to one extra minute of buffer for smoother playback. The five-minute rehearsal below still tests that conservative setting.

Give the picture a quick test first

Start with Chrome or Edge on two laptops. Choose Try a quick test for a moving picture that starts after ten seconds. Choose the room’s Invite your mates and send that private browser invitation. A direct room invitation joins automatically; club invitations first ask you to join the club.

Watch together, then end the test before sharing your own source. The browser will ask which tab, window or screen to share; include tab audio if offered. Your saved club or combined room invitation stays the same when sharing restarts. A video-only invitation belongs to its original capture.

Try the three-laptop rehearsal

Open your club and expand Try it together · three-laptop rehearsal. The organiser chooses the ten-second warm-up and Create & join rehearsal. Copy the displayed private rehearsal invitation yourself and send it to two mates.

Each mate joins the club, labels their laptop and chooses Join current rehearsal. When all three are enrolled, the organiser chooses Start generated picture; enrolled viewers connect automatically. This uses a generated picture without opening desktop sharing or copying your clipboard.

Repeat with 5-minute match delay and allow at least six minutes. A viewer can try its own supplier reconnect. Choose End rehearsal for everyone and Download test report to keep the observations. Device labels and remote readings are self-reported; a browser report cannot independently prove separate laptops or homes. Keep reports private.

Reminders and returning to your club

The Match reminders controls are visible above the club diary. Each person enables them on their own browser, and a planned match is needed. Notifications are optional and best effort, with a once-per-minute scheduler rather than exact-second delivery. Save the club on each browser and allow notifications there. A reminder opens that browser’s saved club; it does not carry the private key. If reminders are unavailable, use Add to calendar. A test request alone does not prove a notification appeared on your device.

Clubs and live chat need an internet connection and the club service. If this copy says the service is not connected, use the quick picture test while deployment is being connected. Installing the app is optional; opening its saved shell offline does not restore a live connection.

Forget club on this browser removes this browser’s saved access. It does not delete the club or remove your mates. Keep a private invitation if you want to return.

If the picture stops

Check the organiser is still sharing and everyone has the current invitation. Some routers, VPNs and firewalls block direct connections, and the introduction service can be unavailable. Try the same Wi-Fi first, then different homes.

If a supplying friend disappears, Same End keeps its saved picture and group clock while looking for another route. Recovery can interrupt playback. If the original source disappears, only the already saved window can play; future picture needs a source online.

One-Mac testing recovered after a relay browser closed in about 17.7 seconds, with one stall during the ten-second quick test. That does not prove reliable delivery between different homes, physical Windows computers or phones. Independent backup captures do not switch seamlessly in this release.

Keep your circle private

Anyone with a complete member or room invitation can forward it. There is no per-person approval or removal list. End and replace a room to replace its room invitation; create a new club to replace club access. Chat names are self-chosen.

Club details, chat and the live invitation are encrypted in your browser. The control service sees timing and reminder-registration metadata; the video travels between the group’s peers. Configured expiry is 400 days after the last organiser update for a club, 24 hours for server chat and 90 days for unrefreshed reminder registrations. Cleanup is eventual.
