Open Same End ↗

1. Try the warm-up

Open Same End on your laptop. Choose Try a quick test. Your picture starts in ten seconds.

2. Bring someone in

Choose Invite your mates. Send the copied invitation to your private group chat. Your friend opens it and joins automatically.

If copying is blocked, copy the selected invitation yourself. No reply files or account needed.

3. Watch together

Watch the player in Same End. Choose Turn sound on if needed. Keep the app open and your laptop awake.

Ready for your own screen? End the test, then choose Share a tab. Match rooms use a five-minute shared delay. Make a new invitation for each new room.

Help for your first test

Begin with Chrome or Edge on two laptops. Try the same Wi-Fi first, then different homes. A physical Windows machine and phones still need checking.

If joining stalls, check the caster is online and the invitation belongs to the current room. Leave and reopen the invitation once. Some routers, VPNs or firewalls prevent direct connections; the connection service can also be unavailable.

Keep invitations private. Anyone with the complete link can attempt to join; end the room to close it. Use content you are entitled to capture and share. Protected video may be unavailable.
